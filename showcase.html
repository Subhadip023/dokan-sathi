Project Overview

Dokan Sathi is a comprehensive, production-grade Shop Management and Point-of-Sale (POS) platform engineered for retail shops, grocery stores, pharmacies, wholesale distributors, and daily essentials merchants. It replaces error-prone paper ledgers with automated billing, inventory control, customer Khata recovery, and real-time net profit & loss calculations.

Key Capabilities & Modules

🛒 High-Speed Point of Sale (POS)

Fast billing counter with keyboard shortcuts, packet and unit size calculations, discount handling, and walk-in or customer-linked receipts. Generates standard full-page A4, compact A5, and dual 2-in-1 (Customer + Merchant copy) printable PDF invoices.

📒 Customer Khata & Credit Tracking

Eliminate lost revenue from uncollected customer debts. Every sale updates running credit balances with clear statuses (Full Paid, Partially Paid, Credit) and 1-click settlement recording.

📦 Inventory & Low-Stock Alerts

Track stock by purchased packets and pieces per packet. Supports dual valuation (Cost Rate vs. Selling Rate). Visual warning badges alert the merchant before essential products run out.

🚚 Wholesale Suppliers & Purchase Orders

Manage distributor contact details, phone numbers, and procurement histories. When incoming purchase orders arrive and are marked "Received", stock counts are auto-credited instantly.

📊 Operational Overhead & Net Profit (P&L)

Comprehensive financial reporting calculating true bottom-line earnings: Gross Revenue minus Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) minus operating overheads (Rent, Electricity, Maintenance, Packaging, Salaries).

🌐 Public Digital Storefront & Catalog

Each store receives a dedicated public online catalog URL (/{dokan:slug}/price-list) to share with buyers on WhatsApp or social media, featuring instant PDF price list downloads.

Technical Architecture

Layer Technology Purpose & Functionality
Backend Framework Laravel 11.x (PHP 8.2+) Robust MVC backend, authentication, database migrations, Eloquent ORM.
Frontend Architecture Inertia.js v2.0 Single Page Application (SPA) speed without building a separate REST/GraphQL API.
UI Framework React 18 & Tailwind CSS 3 Component-driven, responsive layout with mobile-first support.
Asset Bundler Vite 6.x Sub-second Hot Module Replacement (HMR) and optimized production compilation.
PDF Generation barryvdh/laravel-dompdf Server-side PDF generation for A4/A5 invoices and product price catalogs.
Database MySQL 8.0+ Relational data modeling with foreign key constraints, indexes, and transactional consistency.
